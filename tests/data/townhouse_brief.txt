ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN BRIEF: COMPACT TWO-STOREY MODERN HOME
Act as an experienced residential architect, structural designer, and interior designer. Design a complete, realistic two-storey modern house on a strictly limited 18 ft × 20 ft (360 sq. ft.) property.
The objective is to maximize usable living space while creating a visually impressive, comfortable, and functional home.
Do not ignore the lot dimensions or invent additional land. Every architectural decision must respect the available property.
1. Site Constraints and Building Dimensions
Property dimensions:

* Width: 18 feet.
* Depth: 20 feet.
* Total lot area: 360 sq. ft.
* Maximum floors: 2.
* No basement unless explicitly justified as feasible.

Critical requirements:

* Account for front, rear, and side setbacks.
* Determine the actual buildable footprint before designing the house.
* Respect applicable building regulations and fire separation requirements.
* Do not assume the entire lot can be covered by the building.
* If the required setbacks make construction impossible, clearly explain the issue rather than producing an unrealistic floor plan.

Aim for the largest practical two-storey building that can legally fit.
2. Architectural Style
Create a contemporary luxury townhouse-inspired design.
The exterior should feature:

* Modern rectangular architectural forms.
* Warm white or light beige exterior finishes.
* Natural wood accents.
* Dark charcoal architectural details.
* Large black-framed windows where permitted.
* A distinctive modern entrance.
* A clean, attractive roofline.
* Subtle exterior architectural lighting.

The house should look sophisticated and expensive despite its small footprint.
Avoid making it look like a temporary structure or an oversized shipping container.
3. Ground Floor Layout
The ground floor must maximize functionality and minimize wasted circulation space.
Prioritize:

* Compact entrance foyer.
* Open-concept kitchen.
* Small but comfortable living area.
* Dining space integrated into the kitchen.
* One practical bathroom if space permits.
* Integrated storage.
* Staircase designed to minimize its footprint.

Use multifunctional furniture and built-in storage.
The kitchen should feature:

* Modern cabinetry.
* Stone countertops.
* Compact appliances.
* Efficient storage.
* Under-cabinet lighting.

The living area should have:

* Comfortable seating.
* Television or entertainment wall.
* Natural lighting.
* Large windows where permitted.

4. Second Floor Layout
Use the second floor for private living spaces.
Prioritize:

* One primary bedroom.
* One secondary bedroom or flexible office, depending on available space.
* One full bathroom.
* Built-in wardrobes.
* Efficient hallway design.
* Natural lighting.

If two bedrooms cannot comfortably fit, prioritize one genuinely spacious bedroom rather than forcing in undersized rooms.
5. Interior Design
Use a modern minimalist aesthetic.
Colour palette:

* Warm white.
* Natural oak.
* Beige.
* Light grey.
* Matte black accents.

Features:

* Light-coloured flooring.
* Built-in cabinetry.
* Wall-mounted furniture where practical.
* Recessed lighting.
* Large mirrors to create a sense of space.
* Minimal visual clutter.
* Consistent materials between floors.

Make the interior feel larger than its actual dimensions.
6. Exterior and Outdoor Space
Because the lot is extremely small, outdoor space must be treated carefully.
Consider:

* A compact front entrance.
* Vertical landscaping.
* A small rooftop terrace only if structurally and legally feasible.
* Large windows for visual openness.
* Privacy screens.
* Efficient exterior lighting.

Do not invent a large backyard, driveway, or garage that cannot physically fit.
